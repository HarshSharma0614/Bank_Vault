ADMIN DIARY

Name: Marcus Reeves Born: 14-02-1978 School: Los Santos High College: San Andreas State University Wife: Angela Reeves Kids: Jason (9), Maria (6) I don't know why I still keep this thing. Maybe because writing it down is easier than saying it out loud. Angela is the one I chose. She's steady, she's kind, the kids adore her, and I'd never say otherwise where she could hear it. But if I'm honest in these pages the way I promised myself I would be — the real love, the one I measure everything else against, was never her. It was the first one. It's always been the first one.
